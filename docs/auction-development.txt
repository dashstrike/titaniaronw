Testing the workflow
1. Register TEST players for the same card and an accessory.
2. Use Carry Forward to mark one player NEXT BIDDER and give the others on
   that item +1 duck. Their saved ducks remain hidden while next bidder.
3. Use Outbid Again for a later auction round to give the others another +1.
4. Use the green check to mark the item received. This records the receipt,
   removes the registration and gives the remaining players +1 duck.
5. Use Clear Next Bidder or Withdraw when needed; neither awards ducks.
6. Ban a player until a GMT+8 date/time. All their active registrations and
   ducks are removed across categories. Receipt history is preserved.
   After expiry, new registration is allowed and starts at one duck.
7. Ban List shows the global list. Action History includes the admin/time.
8. Add 1 Duck and Remove 1 Duck are available for a player who is not NEXT BIDDER.
   Adding affects only that player's registration for the selected item.
   It can reduce one duck to zero without removing the registration. At zero,
   the player has no race entries until they gain another duck. The adjustment
   appears in Action History and can be undone while it is the latest action.

No auction date needs to be entered. Carry Forward keeps the player as NEXT
BIDDER for that item until the item returns. Outbid Again is used only when
that player is outbid at a later appearance of the same item. Action times
are recorded automatically. Request retries and stale page actions are
rejected to prevent accidental duplicate duck awards. A separately confirmed
Outbid Again action awards the other players another duck.
Undo is available only for the latest action before any newer registration
or admin action. It restores the affected state without overwriting later work.
